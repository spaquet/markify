# Mindmap examples

Every example from Ondine's `site/src/content/docs/diagrams/{name}.mdx`, unchanged.

## Example 1

```mermaid
mindmap
  root((Hawaiʻi))
    History
      Old legends
      ::icon(fa fa-book)
      Voyaging
        Polynesian navigators who sailed by the stars
    Nature
      Mountains<br/>and volcanoes
      Ocean life
        Reef
            Sea turtles
            Monk seals
            Parrotfish
    Culture
      Hula and lei
      ʻUkulele
```
