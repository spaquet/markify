Inspector panel — Contents & Links

Contents lists every heading level, not just # and ##, with tree guides, collapsible sections, a depth control (H1 / H2 / H3 / All) and a filter. The heading you're reading is highlighted, its guide line turns blue, and a progress bar sits at the bottom with an Insert Table of Contents button. Links replaces the tall cards with compact rows grouped into This document / Files / Web, with filter chips and broken-link badges. Hovering a row swaps its line number for actions: go to line, open, and summarize with ✦ (on-device).
